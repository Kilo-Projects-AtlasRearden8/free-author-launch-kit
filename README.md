# Free Author Launch Kit

A free, no-account-required toolkit of fillable, printable worksheets for authors launching a book. Built by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026) and offered alongside Dylan Asdale's beginner-friendly AI education work at [dylanasdale.github.io](https://dylanasdale.github.io).

## What's inside

| Tool | What it does | Files |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Launch Checklist | Step-by-step pre-launch, launch-week, and post-launch tasks with checkboxes | `launch-checklist.html`, `launch-checklist.md` |
| Press Release Template | Fill-in-the-blanks press release ready to send to blogs, newsletters, and media | `press-release.html`, `press-release.md` |
| ARC Reader Tracker | Track advance reader copies sent out and reviews coming back | `arc-reader-tracker.html`, `arc-reader-tracker.md` |
| Review Log | Record reviews, ratings, quotes, and thank-you follow-ups | `review-log.html`, `review-log.md` |
| Social-Post Planner | Plan and schedule posts across platforms with hashtags and links | `social-post-planner.html`, `social-post-planner.md` |

## How to use

1. Open any `.html` file in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
2. Fill in the fields on screen.
3. Press **Ctrl+P** (or **Cmd+P**) to print. The print view strips out buttons and shows clean, fillable worksheets.
4. Save or print as PDF if you'd like a digital copy.

No account, no signup, no internet needed after download. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.
