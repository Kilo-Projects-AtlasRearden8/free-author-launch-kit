Book Launch Checklist

© 2026 Asdale Dynamics, LLC — free, no account needed

A plain-English, step-by-step checklist to take your book from "finished manuscript" to launch day and beyond. Print it out, check boxes off with a pen, and write notes in the margins. Nothing is collected or stored — this runs entirely in your browser.

Before launch — 4 to 6 weeks out

  • Manuscript is edited and proofread
  • Cover design is finalized (ebook, print, and spine)
  • Book blurb and tagline are written
  • ARC reader list is built and copies are sent out
  • Press release is drafted (see Press Release Template)
  • Social media posts are planned (see Social-Post Planner)
  • KDP page is set up (title, description, categories, keywords)
  • Pre-order is enabled (if using one)
  • Email list is told the launch date

Launch week

  • Book is published and live on KDP
  • Launch announcement goes out (email, social, press release)
  • Buy links are shared and working
  • ARC readers are reminded to post reviews
  • Reviews and sales are being logged (see Review Log)

After launch

  • Thank-you notes sent to ARC readers and early reviewers
  • Review log is updated with new reviews and quotes
  • KDP categories and keywords are reviewed and adjusted
  • Any paid promotion or ads are set up and tracked
  • Next promotion or price pulse is planned
